First message

01 Start the chat

Open the DM. The app says hello with a short consent notice — what it stores, how long the reply window lasts, and links to the privacy, terms and data-deletion pages — and notes you have a 24-hour window to agree. Reply YES to begin.

Hi, loveling ✨ — quick hello before we begin. By chatting here you agree to our privacy & terms (linked below). You can say DELETE any time to remove everything. Reply YES within 24 hours to continue.
You
YES
A hello bubble, a leaf-and-sparkle, and your dashed YES that opens the 24-hour window.

If you don’t reply in time, the hello simply lapses — nothing is kept. You can message again later.

Cataloguing

02 Share a single garment

Send a photo of one piece — a top, bottom, dress, cardigan, blazer, scarf or bag. The app catalogues it straight away:

"Got your top — noted, loveling! ✨ It's tucked safely in your wardrobe."

Your piece is saved with the cropped garment photo plus the original, both encrypted at rest.

One garment on a hanger, inside the dashed capture box — one green spark says “catalogued”.

Batch cataloguing

03 Share an outfit photo

One photo can hold several garments. The app finds each piece (up to 6) and asks once:

"Found 3 pieces in that photo, loveling ✨ — a top, a skirt and a bag. Shall I add all of them?"

Reply YES to add them, or no to skip. If you sent multiple photos in one batch, you still get one combined ask — one yes answers it all.

Flat-lay: top, skirt, bag — each in a numbered dashed box. One question, one YES.

Styling

04 Ask for a look

Say “show me a look”. You’ll get a full-look card: which pieces were chosen, why they fit the rules, and a preview image grounded in your actual pieces — or an honest text-only note when no picture could be made.

Your cashmere cardigan + linen trousers + woven tote, loveling ✨ — a soft base with a single warm layer; fit check: valid ✔
Preview grounded in your wardrobe pieces; text-only when no image is available.

Look shape: a base with exactly one finish — a layer (cardigan / blazer) or a scarf + bag pair; a lone scarf or lone bag can finish too. Shoes never join a look.

Three pieces on hooks, stitched into one card — fit check ✔ and a sparkle.

Tweaking

05 Change the look

Just say it naturally:

“without the blazer please” · “swap the cardigan for the blazer” · “add my scarf”

The look re-composes and the fit rules re-check. If you ask to remove the only finish, the app will tell you what’s missing rather than showing a broken look.

One hanger out (dashed arrow), another in — the fit is checked again.

On-you preview

06 “Can you show it on me?”

Ask to see the look on you. The app will first ask for consent, then for two reference photos one at a time: a headshot and a full-body photo. They’re kept encrypted, used only to render looks on you, and deletable any time with “delete my photos”. Your headshot is never catalogued as a wardrobe piece.

To show a look on you, darling ✨ I’ll keep just two photos — a headshot and a full-body — encrypted, only to render your looks on you and never for anything else. Say “delete my photos” anytime to remove them. May I keep them? A quick “yes” and I’ll ask for your headshot first.
Wardrobe → you → your mirror reflection. Dashed arrow, same paper and ink.

Browsing

07 Ask the wardrobe

Say “what do I have in my wardrobe?” for a summary by category. Or be pointed:

“delete my dress” · “the pink one” · “view my scarf”

The app will show pages you can step through, confirm before deleting, and remember what you picked.

Open wardrobe — folded piles, hanging rails, and a dashed pointer finding one shelf.

Prospective piece

08 “Should I buy this?”

Share a photo of something you’re considering. The app checks how it would complete your existing looks and can hold it for you for 24 hours. If you decide to buy, just say “I bought it” and it’s filed into your wardrobe.

Ooh, let’s have a look, loveling ✨ I’ll hold this one just for 24 hours while you decide — from what you’ve got, this could unlock 2 lovely outfits. Say “I bought it” to catalogue it properly, or I’ll gently let it go.
A thought bubble over the bag you’re eyeing — the little 24h clock is ticking.

Privacy

09 Your data, your delete

At any point you can say DELETE — or “delete my photos” / “forget me” — and the app purges your wardrobe, photos and history: everything, verified. Other users’ data is untouched.

Wardrobe lid closing, padlock shut — one leaf drifts away. Only your data goes.
What “delete” does: removes your wardrobe, stored photos, history and held pieces, verified. Saying it once is enough — and it never touches anyone else’s wardrobe.
Finish rule: a base + one finish — a layer, a scarf+bag pair, or a lone scarf / bag — shoes stay out of fits.
